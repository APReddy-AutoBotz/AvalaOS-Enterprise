# Checking and paying a claim (made-up example for tests)

This procedure is invented for Avala OS's own tests. It is not published by anyone.

## Receive the claim

The claims officer receives the claim form by post or online.

The claims officer checks that the form is signed and dated.

## Pay the claim

If the claim is complete, the payments officer pays it within 10 working days.

If the claim is not complete, the claims officer writes to the person and asks for what is missing.
